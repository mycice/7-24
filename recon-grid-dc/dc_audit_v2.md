- theta_dot guard threshold and the d-floor are coupled in a way that makes the guard's apparent eps a no-op label rather than a tunable. Line 250 computes d=sqrt(max(1-c*c,1e-8)), which floors d to exactly 1e-4 (=sqrt(1e-8)); line 251 then guards `if d<=1e-4: continue`. So the guard fires iff the floor activated (1-c*c < 1e-8, i.e. |c| within ~5e-5 of 1). The two magic numbers 1e-8 and 1e-4 MUST stay in lock-step: if a future tweak raises the floor (e.g. 1e-6) without raising the guard, d can never reach <=1e-4 and the guard becomes dead code, silently re-admitting near-singular pairs and large theta_dot. - Ablation Pass2 'ablated edge' set vs the cut-bit/partial-quad path has an unhandled boundary case for an ablated edge whose far endpoint is a SURVIVING (alive) particle. §5.9 Pass2 marks edges 'connecting ablated particles, including edges leading to surviving neighbors (C27)' and generates cut points on them, then writes them into voxelCutMaskBuf. This determines whether the surviving side gets a closed cut surface.
  FIX: In §5.5/§5.9 state explicitly that ablated-edge cut points are accumulated into the SURVIVING endpoint's component centroid (the deleted endpoint contributes none, since its corner is absent), and add an EditMode case: a single ablated particle adjacent to alive neighbors yields a closed cut surface on the alive side.
