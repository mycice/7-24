## Native Stage 4 — §2.1.2 dynamic cutting: MATERIAL-SPACE RIBBON detection (plan 2026-07-02)

**Problem (proven, 3-subagent diagnosis wf_e3f28151 all CONFIRMED):** gravity=0 cuts perfectly; gravity=-4
leaves holes along the path (COMPONENTS=7, tiny fragments). Root cause = space-time undersampling of
world-frame detection: `LCS_Step` moves tissue ONCE per frame, then rod substeps detect with tissue FROZEN
(LiverCudaManager.cs:270-293); the swept quad covers only the TOOL's motion, never the tissue's. CPU proof
`scratchpad/tunnel_repro.py`: dense world sampling at 1/5/30/100/300× NEVER separates (comps=1, non-monotonic)
— the plane-crossing coincidence point races along edges faster than the blade; unfixable by substepping.
Zero-g exact 3-way equality explains the user's 0-g-perfect evidence. D3 ruled out all alternatives
(capacity/POS_SCALE/atomicOr/EdgeIsCut/AABB/dt).

**Fix (paper §2.1.2 realized literally — "the edge has not undergone any deformation"):**
1. Per detection event, sample the rod segment (S=t2v2, E=t1v2 from CutToolDesc; ~0.5·L spacing, ≤256).
2. Map each sample world→MATERIAL via the continuous inverse of the hex embedding: rod-AABB broadphase →
   deformed-voxel AABB cull → inverse-trilinear Newton (8 iters); deterministic voxel claim
   (occupied-preferred, most-interior |uvw−½|∞ tie-break — kills the multi-voxel ambiguity D2-M5);
   invalid samples: offset-extrapolate from nearest valid; none valid: last-known offset (aged ≤16) else skip.
3. Swept MATERIAL ribbon = strip between the PREVIOUS sample state and current (2·(NS−1) tris).
4. k_DetectCut: ray = UNDEFORMED REST edge (uniform lattice ⇒ marks tile); tris = ribbon; on hit t:
   alongRest = t·L·axis (existing §2.1.2 EmitCutPoint recording), world P_hit = posA+t·(posB−posA) for the
   ±D/2 side; world-proximity clamp (hit within 3L of the rod segment) kills cross-lobe phantom cuts (D2-M7).
5. **Temporal ticks (D2-blocker fix):** `physics_step` calls `cut_ribbon_tick()` after EVERY accepted RK45
   substep (rod fixed, tissue moved → ribbon extends); the C# rod substeps cover rod motion (tissue frozen).
   Together they tile the full space-time surface at the finest granularity that exists in the simulator.
   CPU proof `scratchpad/tick_validation.py` (D2's OSC=4 attack regime): per-frame ribbon aliases; TICK=8 → ~0 holes.
6. Detection gate = ribbon non-degeneracy (NOT tool.valid) ⇒ stationary rod vs falling tissue cuts (D2-M3).
7. Downstream UNCHANGED: mask/conn4096/sever/cut-FP chain/[22] Fig-4 frames; zero-deformation invariant:
   identity map ⇒ ribbon == world swept quad ⇒ behavior identical to the working 0-g cut.

**Fidelity note:** §2.1.3's swept plane (2 world tris) is kept as the SAME plane expressed in material
coordinates and time-refined at RK45 substeps; §2.1.2's undeformed-edge recording + particle-axes
reconstruction unchanged. The paper is silent on tissue motion during cutting (its demos are zero-g);
this is the coherent completion, documented as a deviation.
